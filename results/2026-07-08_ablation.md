## Stage 2 — combinations & star-graph spot checks (complete, 3 seeds)

| cell | task | decision_acc | path_acc | erank | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `abl_s2_combo` (c4 + InfoNCE + EMA tgt4) | maze | .746 ± .027 | .673 ± .018 | 52.4 | **no stacking** — equals c4 alone (.746) |
| `abl_s2_mtp8jepa` (MTP k=8 + pooled EMA tgt4) | maze | .795 ± .009 | .747 ± .019 | 27.5 | **interference** — MTP k=8 alone is .845; adding the latent loss *costs* .05 |
| `abl_s2_sg_mtp8` (MTP k=8) | star graph | .190 ± .019 | .190 ± .019 | 30.0 | still exactly chance (0.2) |
| `abl_s2_sg_combo` (best latent combo, k=5) | star graph | .198 ± .026 | .198 ± .026 | 9.6 | still exactly chance |

Three sharp conclusions:

1. **The mild latent wins do not add** — combo ≡ its best single ingredient.
2. **MTP and the latent loss interfere when sharing a trunk at the same
   depth** (.845 → .795; erank drops toward MTP's collapsed regime). This is
   direct evidence for depth-separating the two objectives (D2′) rather than
   just summing them.
3. **Star graph resists even full-horizon discrete supervision**: MTP heads
   spanning the entire 7-token answer stay at chance. Maze junctions are
   local (few-token lookahead) — that's what MTP exploits; star graph's first
   decision requires whole-arm lookahead, and *no* auxiliary loss tried fixes
   teacher-forced training there. Consistent with Bachmann & Nagarajan, whose
   fix was changing the *training scheme* (teacherless), not the loss.

4. **Correction to the interim read**: the step-1200 snapshot (d2v25 at .53)
   that looked like a breakout was not ahead of pace — clean NTP passes .53

### 3c. Repair cells — removing the NTP contamination (complete, 3 seeds)

Both keep p=.25 corruption for the latent-loss source; they differ in how
NTP is protected. Bar to clear: clean-JEPA .744.

| cell | NTP protection | decision_acc | path_acc | erank | aux @2k/@4k |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `abl_h_d2v25m` | loss masked where predecessor corrupted (2 passes) | .583 ± .007 | .458 ± .013 | 27.2 | 0.070 / 0.039 |
| `abl_h_d2v25c` | NTP on a fully clean pass; corrupted pass feeds only the latent source (3 passes) | **.733 ± .044** | .652 ± .031 | 40.8 | 0.049 / 0.057 |

5. **Masking ill-posed targets does not repair the damage** (d2v25m .583 ≈
   d2v25 .589). So the harm is not (mainly) the unlearnable loss terms — it
   is training NTP *in a corrupted-context distribution* at all: every
   position's context contains masked tokens, and eval is clean. Skipping
   ~p of targets removes the label noise but keeps the distribution shift.
6. **The clean-NTP 3-pass fully repairs but does not improve** (d2v25c
   .733 ± .044 ≈ clean JEPA .744 ± .020), at +30% step time. The
   decontaminated corrupted-source latent task *is* measurably harder (aux
   holds 0.05–0.06 nats vs ~0.02 clean) — and it still buys nothing on the
   task metric. Corrupted-context latent prediction has no standalone value
   here.

### Round-3 bottom line (final)

Every hardness axis tried — target gap, discrete codes, hard negatives,
entropy-weighted loss mass, and input corruption in five variants including
both NTP-decontaminated repairs — lands at or below the latent family's .74
plateau. Two of these provably kept the aux loss hard to the end and still
didn't help. MTP k=8 (.845) remains +.10 ahead, untouched. The surviving
hypothesis for latent-loss planning gains on Tier 0 is now a single item:
**depth-separated MTP+latent stacks (D2′)**, motivated by the Stage-2
interference result (.845 → .795 when they share a trunk at the same depth).

## Project-level verdict after rounds 1–4

On Tier-0 planning at this scale, the latent-prediction auxiliary family
has been tested alone (r1), stacked with itself (s2), hardened five ways
(r3a), input-corrupted five ways incl. NTP-decontaminated (r3b/c), and
depth-separated from MTP at three placements/weights (r4): **its reliable
effect is representational (erank), never behavioral (planning), and when
combined with MTP it strictly subtracts.** The discrete-target control (MTP)
is the only auxiliary loss that moved planning, and only on the task whose
decisions are local (maze); star graph resists everything. Remaining moves
are *scheme*-level, not loss-level: teacherless/lookahead training
(Bachmann–Nagarajan), or moving the fight to Tier-1 where the erank benefit
could plausibly pay off in transfer rather than planning.

## Round 5 — synonym-rendered maze (complete, 3 seeds)

Testbed pivot (user diagnosis): maze/stargraph have one-to-one emission —
token space *is* the semantic space — so token CE is already the maximally
informative signal. Round 5 makes emission many-to-one: each cell gets s
surface synonyms, sampled i.i.d. per occurrence; eval scores equivalence
classes. Token CE acquires an irreducible log(s)/token floor (1.39 nats at
s=4, 2.77 at s=16); the latent path stays deterministic. s=1 anchors are the
existing runs (task bit-identical).

decision_acc (maze 6×6, 4000 steps, 3 seeds):

| method | s=1 (anchor) | s=4 | s=16 | s=16 answer-only |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ntp | .696 ± .043 | .721 ± .008 | .682 ± .015 | .682 ± .012 |
| mtp k=8 | .845 ± .025 | .844 ± .055 | **.846 ± .019** | .849 ± .042 |
| jepa (pooled k8 EMA tgt4) | .744 ± .020 | .719 ± .027 | .724 ± .024 | .729 ± .012 |

path_acc shows the same pattern (mtp8 .807–.820 at every s; ntp .590–.646;
jepa .642–.652). Synonym-invariance probe (embedding cosine): within-class
.88–.93 vs between-class ≈ 0.00 in **every** cell and method.

**Verdicts:**
1. **Token-level nuisance does not separate the methods.** MTP is exactly
   flat across s (.845 → .846 at s=16 — 2.77 nats/token of target noise,
   zero planning cost). NTP and JEPA are flat within noise. No crossover.
2. **Mechanism: the nuisance is absorbed at the embedding layer.** The
   probe shows every method learns near-perfect synonym clustering
   (within .9 / between .0) from NTP pressure alone; past the embedding,
   the network sees the class and the log(s) floor is a constant loss
   offset, not gradient noise where it matters.
3. The answer-only variant ≈ the everywhere variant for all methods — the
   context-binding burden is also negligible.
4. This confirms the user's concern that token-level invariance is
   defeatable by lookup, and sharpens the requirement: the nuisance must be
   structural (e.g., order invariance, round 6), not absorbable by a
   per-token re-encoding.

## Round 6 — top-3 longest paths, order-invariant answers (complete, 3 seeds)

Task: emit the 3 longest paths of a 5×5 maze tree, any order, either
direction (nuisance log(3!·2³) ≈ 3.9 nats at path boundaries); syn16 row
composes token-level nuisance on top (2.77 nats/token). Eval: set_acc =
all 3 paths valid+simple+distinct with lengths matching the gold top-3
multiset. Difficulty pilots: tp1_5x5 .941 (saturated), tp1_6x6 .840,
tp3_5x5 .809 → grid on tp3_5x5. NTP anchor for context: unique-path maze
6×6 ntp .696 / mtp8 .845 / jepa .744.

set_acc (4000 steps, 3 seeds):

| method | tp3 (order nuisance only) | tp3 + syn16 (compositional) | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| ntp | .793 ± .022 | .673 ± .021 | −.120 |
| mtp k=8 | .801 ± .041 | **.746 ± .025** | −.055 |
| jepa (pooled k8 EMA tgt4) | .793 ± .017 | .697 ± .016 | −.096 |

path_valid .95–.97 and len_ratio .99+ everywhere; syn_cos_within ≥ .95 in
all syn16 cells (token-level quotient again learned perfectly).

**Verdicts:**
1. **Order nuisance alone: null — and it neutralizes MTP.** All three
   methods land at .79–.80. MTP's big unique-path edge (+.15 over NTP on
   maze) vanishes: its k-step heads straddle path boundaries, where the
   marginal over token t+k is a mixture across order/direction choices —
   the discrete-target advantage dissolves into that mixture. JEPA's
   "cleaner pooled target" buys nothing over NTP.
2. **Compositional nuisance (order × synonyms) is the first nuisance that
   hurts at all** — everyone drops, even though each factor alone was free
   (round 5: syn16 −.00; here: order −.00). The two quotients don't
   compose cheaply.
3. **Ranking under compositional nuisance: MTP > JEPA > NTP.** MTP is the
   *most* robust (−.055), not the least; JEPA sits between (−.096, ~1.3σ
   above NTP — same mild edge it had on plain maze). The prediction
   "latent degrades less under nuisance" is falsified in the strongest
   available regime: tp3+syn16 is by construction the setting where the
   pooled latent target is strictly cleaner than token targets
   (deterministic semantics, encoder-invariant pooled mean), and the
   discrete CE auxiliary still wins.
4. Theory note (see discussion 2026-07-10): this extends the conditional-
   dominance law — on Tier-0 tasks with deterministic semantics, every
   nuisance tried is either absorbed (embedding lookup, order bookkeeping)
   or degrades token CE less than latent regression. The latent family's
   case now rests entirely on regimes Tier 0 cannot instantiate:
   irreducible semantic entropy at long horizon, and representation
   transfer — both Tier-1 questions.

## Round 7 — gridembed: inverse maze layout (complete, 3 seeds, 2026-07-11)

Task: given the shuffled edge list of a maze tree over randomly relabeled
vertices, emit the full (2H−1)×(2W−1) drawing (vertex labels + explicit
E/W edge slots + C filler) from a random D4 perspective. Verification-based
eval on held-out mazes: embed_acc = permutation valid ∧ stated E set ==
input edge set exactly. No copy shortcut; greedy baselines: naive 0.000,
degree-pruned 3% (4×4) / 0.1% (5×5) per rollout.

Difficulty pilots (NTP, 1 seed): 4×4 final embed .031 / edge_f1 .756
(off-floor, graded); 5×5 final embed .000 / edge_f1 .076 (floor) → grid
at 4×4 + mtp8-only floor-lift check at 5×5.

4000 steps, 3 seeds (runs abl_ge{4,5}_{ntp,mtp8,jepa}_seed{0,1,2}):

| cell | embed_acc final | embed_acc best | edge_f1 final | edge_f1 best | perm_valid final | erank_last |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ge4 ntp | .026 ± .027 | .040 ± .035 | .533 ± .343 | .564 ± .358 | .478 ± .439 | 8 ± 1 |
| ge4 mtp k=8 | **.141 ± .076** | **.203 ± .082** | **.851 ± .028** | **.878 ± .022** | .503 ± .158 | 29 ± 12 |
| ge4 jepa (pooled k8 EMA tgt4) | .048 ± .022 | .065 ± .019 | .777 ± .026 | .793 ± .010 | .271 ± .076 | 22 ± 4 |
| ge5 mtp k=8 | .007 ± .005 | .014 ± .006 | .783 ± .032 | .795 ± .028 | .108 ± .040 | 40 ± 6 |

(5×5 NTP reference from pilot: embed .000, edge_f1 .076.)

**Verdicts:**
1. **NTP learns exactly the myopic policy.** Its trajectory has two
   phases: first perm_valid → 1.0 with edge_f1 ~.15 (well-formed, wrong),
   then a transition where it trades permutation validity for local edge
   satisfaction (edge_f1 → .76, perm_valid → .24) and plateaus at
   embed_acc 3–5% — the greedy-with-pruning baseline. One seed (ntp_seed2)
   never makes the transition (edge_f1 .137 at 4000 steps), hence the
   huge NTP variance. The gap to 100% is a lookahead gap, and NTP does
   not close any of it.
2. **MTP k=8 gets 3–5× the full-solution rate** (best .203 vs .040) and
   dominates edge_f1 (.878 vs .564). Unlike tp3 (order mixtures neutralized
   its heads), here every future token is a deterministic function of the
   sampled perspective+labels prefix, so k-step targets are clean — and
   pressure to predict 8 tokens ahead is pressure to place vertices whose
   consequences surface within the row. MTP's advantage returns precisely
   when the task is planning-limited rather than nuisance-limited.
3. **Graded floor-lift at 5×5** — the anti-star-graph. NTP at 5×5 learns
   almost nothing (edge_f1 .076); MTP lifts edge_f1 to .78 (≈ NTP's 4×4
   level) though full solutions stay ~1%. Star graph's cliff hid this
   regime: with a graded metric, lookahead training demonstrably extracts
   structure where NTP cannot start.
4. **JEPA is again strictly between NTP and MTP** (.065 best embed, edge_f1
   .793) — same +small edge over NTP as rounds 1–6, far below the discrete
   auxiliary, with the usual representational signature (erank 22 vs 8).
   On the first genuinely planning-limited Tier-0 task, latent pooled
   prediction still does not beat token-space MTP: conditional dominance
   holds in the Bachmann–Nagarajan regime too.

Double null: continuous ≈ pause ≈ ntp (0/6 each), all at/below chance,
while mtp8 solves 6/6 at the same schedule. Unsupervised latent thought
slots — even causal, recurrent ones the model conditions on at answer
time — do not break the teacher-forcing shortcut: with the answer still
teacher-forced token-by-token after EOT, the per-position loss is
minimized by the local-follow shortcut without ever consulting the
thoughts, so no gradient pressure ever puts a plan into them. Consistent
with Coconut needing its staged CoT curriculum (explicit supervision of
what the thoughts replace) and with Pfau et al.'s filler-token limits.

Project law after 16 rounds, unchanged and sharpened: planning emerges
exactly when supervision cannot be satisfied without the plan (multi-token
bottleneck heads); extra latent capacity, latent targets, latent teachers,
and latent computation all fail without that pressure. Natural next cell
(not launched): thoughts + MTP heads reading the thought states — replace
Coconut's curriculum with the bottleneck pressure that works here.
