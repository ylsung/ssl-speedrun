+.01 shallow): **EMA matters because it makes deep targets usable**; the
depth curve under EMA peaks at ⅔ depth.

## Q6 — objective / anti-collapse family, at EMA tgt=4, k=8

| alignment | regularizer | run | decision_acc | path_acc | erank |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cosine (ref) | none | `maze2_jepa_ema` | .744 ± .020 | .663 ± .018 | 51.1 |
| L2 | none | `abl_l2_ema_tgt4` | .742 ± .033 | .673 ± .024 | 52.0 |
| L2 | SIGReg | `abl_sigreg_ema_tgt4` | .706 ± .029 | .621 ± .028 | 54.7 |
| L2 | VICReg | `abl_vicreg_ema_tgt4` | .729 ± .020 | .652 ± .010 | 57.7 |
| InfoNCE τ=0.1 | none | `abl_infonce_ema_tgt4` | **.750 ± .037** | .667 ± .029 | **85.1** |

InfoNCE is nominally best and roughly doubles effective rank (85 vs ~50) —
the healthiest representation geometry of any run in the project — but is
within noise of cosine on the task metric. SIGReg *hurts* on this task
(opposite of LeJEPA's vision results); plausibly a Gaussian latent prior is
wrong for a 42-token synthetic task, or the regularizer weight needs tuning.
Re-test on text before writing it off.

## Bottom line

Best latent cells: `abl_infonce_ema_tgt4` .750 ± .037 and `abl_c4_ema_tgt4`
.746 ± .015. Discrete control at the same horizon: `abl_mtp_k8`
**.845 ± .025**. After a 22-cell sweep the latent family remains ~.10 behind:
on Tier-0 planning at this scale its reliable benefit is **representational
(erank), not behavioral**. The hypothesis-3 kill-gate verdict stands.
