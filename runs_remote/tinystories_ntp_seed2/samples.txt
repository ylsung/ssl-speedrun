 Once upon a time there was an ordinary man who lived in the ocean. He loved to go to the beach and watch the waves. Every day he would take a fishing trip with his net.

One day while he was fishing, he noticed something special. It was a bright blue shell! He carefully picked it up and took it home to show his family. They were so surprised by how quickly he had found it!

The man carefully put the shell in his net, so he could take it out every day. He liked to keep it and show it to his family.

The man liked to go fishing every day. He knew he could catch lots of fish and keep them in the big blue sea. He was so happy to have the shell in his net.

 Once upon a time there was a little boy called Tom. Tom was very sad, because his friends were being very mean to him. Tom didn't know what to do, so he just sat there, feeling very alone.

One day, Tom heard a noise outside. He stepped out of his house and saw a group of kids playing in the park. Tom wanted to join in, but he was scared. He asked the kids, "Why are you being so mean to me?"

The kids laughed and said, "We were just playing hide-and-seek. We wanted to surprise you!"

Tom felt so relieved, he forgot all about being mean. He said, "Thank you, but I just wanted to join in too!" 

The kids smiled and said, "We can all play together! It'll be more fun!"

Tom said, "Yay! Let's go!"

And so Tom and the kids spent the day playing tag and having lots of fun. Tom felt happy, and he realized that he didn't have to be so mean to anyone. He was happy that his friends were happy too.

 It was a beautiful day when the sun began to rise. The ground was so soft and the sky was so blue.

Suddenly, the little girl heard something. She looked up and saw a big bird in the sky. It was so beautiful! She wanted to touch it.

So she opened her window and asked the bird if she could come and see it. The bird said yes and flew out onto the field. The little girl ran towards the bird when it came back.

The little girl was so happy and enjoyed the view of the bird. She thought the bird was the prettiest thing she had ever seen!
